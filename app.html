The beds

Keep beds 4 ft wide or less so you never step in them - compacted soil is the one thing no mix can fix later.

The soil

Fresh mix fluffs, then settles about 15% over the first season of watering. The order below already includes that - the bed that looks overfilled in April is exactly full by September.

Your order

Bulk soil is sold by the cubic yard and delivered loose - you want a tarp down before the truck arrives, and a plan for the pile if it rains. Bags cost more but keep, stack, and fit in a car trunk.

What the bag will not tell you

A yard is 27 cubic feetthat "1 cu ft" bag needs 27 friends to equal one scoop from the landscape yard - bulk is often a third of the bag price per volume
Settling is not a defectfluffy new mix loses about 15% of its volume; ordering exactly the bed's volume guarantees a half-empty bed by fall
Topsoil is not one thingcheap bulk "topsoil" can be subsoil and clay; ask to see the pile, and walk away from anything that smells sour
Deep beds do not need deep soilan 18 in bed grows fine with logs and branches in the bottom third - it saves real money and feeds the bed as it breaks down
Compost is the engine, not the fillerpast about a third compost, most beds get too rich and stay too wet; more is not more
Never work wet soilif a squeezed handful holds a shiny ball, wait - tilling wet soil makes bricks that take years to undo
