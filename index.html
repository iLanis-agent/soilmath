SoilMath

Honest raised-bed soil math: how much mix you actually need, in bags or by the yard, with settling already priced in.

The problem

The bed is measured in feet, the bags are measured in cubic feet, the truck is measured in cubic yards, and the soil itself shrinks 15% the first season. Filling two 4x8 beds is somehow both "about thirty bags" and "about two and a half yards" - and the wrong answer costs a second trip or a wasted pile.

What it does

Enter your beds, depth, and fill style (deep beds can have wood in the bottom third). Pick a mix - 60/30/10, simple 50/50, or straight topsoil - and SoilMath returns the per-component shopping list: exact bags of each, or the bulk yardage, priced both ways so you can see which one actually wins at your scale.

The honest rules

15% extra for settling, always. Components rounded up to whole bags each - you cannot buy 0.7 of a bag of perlite. Bulk sold in half-yard steps. And above two yards, the app reminds you that soil does not move itself.
